Halomux

Welcome

Halomux makes your Philips Hue lamps follow your screen. The setup takes about two minutes. You need:

  • A Hue bridge in the same network as this computer
  • Access to the bridge, to press its button
  • The Hue app, to set up an entertainment area with your lamps

Find your bridge

Pick your Hue bridge. If it isn't found, enter its IP address: the Hue app shows it under Settings → My Hue system → Bridge.

The identity of this bridge can't be checked

Its certificate isn't signed by the Philips Hue certification authority. Older bridges have such certificates, but so would another device in your network passing itself off as your bridge: paired with it, that device could control your Hue system.

Only go on if this is the address of your Hue bridge, as the Hue app shows it under Settings → My Hue system → Bridge.

Pair with the bridge

Press the round button on top of your Hue bridge now.

The app pairs as soon as the button is pressed, you don't need to click anything here.

Entertainment area

The lamps are driven through an entertainment area of the bridge, which you set up in the Hue app.

No entertainment area was found yet.

  1. Open the Hue app and go to Settings → Entertainment areas.
  2. Add an area, for example "PC", and select the lamps around your screen.
  3. Place the lamps where they stand relative to the screen.

This page checks again every few seconds.

Ready

Next, the desktop asks which screens Halomux may capture. Tick every monitor a lamp should follow, and allow it to be remembered, so you aren't asked again on every start.

Then, in the light manager:

  1. Drag your lamps from Available to Active.
  2. Select a lamp and adjust the part of the screen it follows.
  3. Save the profile.
